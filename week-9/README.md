# Week 9 — Career Preparation and Hiring Guide

## Project Overview

This week's group assignment involved creating a Data Analyst Hiring Guide for UrbanStyle.ltd. The purpose was to help the company's management identify suitable candidates using clear and practical hiring criteria.

## My Role — C: People Manager

I focused on four areas:

- **Communication:** Explaining technical findings clearly and listening to others.
- **Problem-solving:** Investigating unexpected results and finding solutions.
- **Teamwork:** Sharing knowledge, communicating progress and supporting colleagues.
- **Cultural fit:** Respecting different perspectives, accepting feedback and showing willingness to improve.

I developed three green flags, two red flags, a practical interview question and a recommendation to help UrbanStyle assess candidates fairly.

## Advanced Contribution

I also prepared a hiring scorecard using a 1–5 rating system, evidence-based comments and hiring recommendations.

The scorecard encourages managers to assess candidates based on demonstrated abilities rather than personality or confidence alone.

## Key Learning

I learned that hiring a strong data analyst involves more than checking technical knowledge. Communication, critical thinking, collaboration and willingness to learn are also important.

A structured hiring process can help managers make fairer and more consistent decisions.
