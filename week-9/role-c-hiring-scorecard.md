## 7. Fair Assessment Reminder

A candidate should not receive a higher score simply because they appear confident or outgoing.

The interviewer should focus on demonstrated skills, relevant examples and willingness to learn.

Disagreement with a manager is not automatically a red flag. What matters is how the disagreement was handled.

Scores should be supported by comments, and missing evidence should be investigated rather than treated automatically as poor performance.
