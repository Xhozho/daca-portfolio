## My Speaking Notes

When we first started looking at UrbanStyle's data, we wanted to understand what was really happening in the business, not just whether the sales numbers looked good.

We worked with sales, customer, product and inventory data, checking for missing information and inconsistencies before analysing the results.

One of our biggest findings was that UrbanStyle's revenue grew by **19.1% in 2024** compared with 2023. That was encouraging, but it also raised another question: where was that growth coming from?

When we looked at customer spending, we found something interesting: just **206 VIP customers generated 31.25% of customer revenue**. That showed us how valuable a relatively small group of customers could be.

Online sales also stood out, with **37.9% revenue growth in 2024**. But it wasn't all positive news. Our inventory analysis identified **231 records at or below their reorder points**, highlighting stock levels that needed attention.

One finding that really surprised us was Tartu. Instead of showing a decline, our analysis revealed that revenue had actually increased by **13% in 2024**.

For me, that was one of the biggest lessons from this project: sometimes the data tells a completely different story from what we expect.
