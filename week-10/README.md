## Overview

Week 10 brings together the work we've completed throughout DACA. Our goal is to present UrbanStyle's most important findings to the board, focusing on what the data revealed and how those insights could support better business decisions.

Our team prepared a seven-minute presentation covering data analysis, visualisations, business recommendations and the use of AI.

## My Contribution — SQL & Data Overview

I took responsibility for preparing the two-minute Data Overview section.

I reviewed our individual GitHub portfolios and previous team reports to identify the strongest findings from the programme.

My focus was on:

- Selecting meaningful numerical findings from our analysis.
- Checking that results were supported by our previous work.
- Explaining what the figures mean for UrbanStyle.
- Identifying important limitations and differences between analyses.
- Connecting my section to the team's visualisation presentation.

## Key Findings

| Finding | Business Significance |
|---|---|
| Overall revenue increased by **19.1% in 2024** | Shows positive year-on-year business growth. |
| **206 VIP customers** generated **31.25% of customer revenue** | Highlights the value of retaining high-spending customers. |

| **231 inventory records** were at or below their reorder points | Highlights stock levels requiring review. |
| Tartu revenue increased by **13% in 2024** | Demonstrates why business assumptions should be checked against data. |

These results come from different stages of the programme. The growth figures compare 2024 with 2023, while customer segmentation and inventory findings use their respective analysis datasets.

## Challenges and Lessons Learned

One challenge was selecting findings from different weeks without mixing up reporting periods, definitions or analytical methods.

For example, our SQL customer segmentation and Python RFM analysis identified different VIP customer groups because they used different classification methods.

I learned that presenting data is not just about choosing impressive numbers. It's also about understanding what those numbers actually prove, recognising their limitations and explaining why they matter.

## AI Use

I used ChatGPT to help review findings, improve the clarity of my presentation, and prepare for possible questions from the board.

I also used AI to challenge my interpretations and understand technical concepts more clearly. The final findings were selected from our documented project work rather than accepting AI-generated numbers without checking their sources.
